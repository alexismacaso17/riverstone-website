Our People

The people behind the places.

David Atchison

Founder

With over 40 years as a developer, broker, investor, consultant, and trusted advisor, David’s mission is to create the maximum value of every property for every client.

Experience

His work spans every asset class — retail, office, industrial, residential, and mixed-use — giving him the range to craft creative strategies for clients from small companies to large institutions. As founder of The Riverstone Group, David is energized by creating places where companies and individuals flourish through both the science and art of real estate development.

Outside the Office

David and his wife Elaine live in Franklin, TN; they have three adult children and a grandson. His break dancing days are over, but he still rocks a mean robot.

Ryan Musser

Director of Development

Ryan brings more than 15 years in project management, residential and commercial construction, and lease negotiations to the development process.

Experience

His hands-on experience, analytical mindset, and attention to detail bring accuracy and confident leadership to every project. A great listener who genuinely cares about people, Ryan builds authentic relationships across the industry and sees complex projects through to completion.

Outside the Office

Ryan and his wife Sarah live in Franklin, TN, with their four children. In his spare time he loves the outdoors, watching football, and projects around the house.

Joel Atchison

Development & Marketing

Joel brings a knowledge and passion for sustainability to The Riverstone Group, grounded in a B.A. in Sustainable Development from Appalachian State University.

Experience

His work ranges from building a solar-powered biodiesel generator that still fuels ASU’s buses to co-founding a Nashville music festival — featured in The New York Times and Forbes as the “greenest” in North America. Joel blends creative energy and marketing with a natural gift for connecting people, helping clients reach their goals while shaping communities built for social well-being, environmental stewardship, and lasting impact.

Drew Cressman

Outside the Office
